## Description
Policy question: What public health issues do unincorporated communities face?
I am hoping to show that isolation and lack of investment lead to higher 
mortality and lower health outcomes. I also hope to show that unincorporated 
communities near a major metropolitian area still face structural human rights 
issues and difficulties meeting basic needs.
